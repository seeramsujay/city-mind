# CityMind: Future Roadmap & Architectural Evolution

CityMind delivers sovereign, multi-agent urban intelligence with immutable state commits, hierarchical memory buffers, and Gemini RAG reasoning. This document details future architectural expansions.

---

## 1. 3D Spatial Digital Twin & WebGL Simulation

- **CesiumJS / Three.js 3D Digital Twin**:
  - Render an interactive 3D geospatial digital twin of city infrastructure directly in the frontend dashboard.
  - Map live sensor telemetry, traffic bottlenecks, and air quality plumes as volumetric 3D heatmaps over real municipal building meshes.
- **Dynamic Emergency Preemption Corridors**:
  - Automatically calculate green-light travel corridors for ambulances and fire engines using real-time GPS feeds and actuated traffic signal controllers.

---

## 2. Autonomous Agent Protocols & Consensus

- **Multi-Agent Negotiation Protocols (FIPA-ACL)**:
  - Formulate structured negotiation protocols between competing city departments (e.g. water distribution vs fire suppression demand vs electrical peak load shedding).
  - Implement zero-knowledge verification for citizen privacy metrics during urban demographic surveys.
- **Hierarchical Warm/Cold Memory Compaction**:
  - Automated vector summarization converting multi-week IoT streams into long-term strategic urban planning documents stored on IPFS / cold S3.

---

## 3. Municipal Interoperability & Open City Standards

- **FIWARE & OASC Open API Standards**:
  - Support NGSI-LD (Next Generation Service Interfaces - Linked Data) to enable turnkey integration with global smart city standards and EU Open Agile Smart Cities data spaces.
